Monday, September 7 · Labor Day

Choose your kind of scenic.

Three moderate options, each with a different payoff: lake views, rocky mountain scenery, or a lush river and waterfall walk. We’ll choose one together and be home for the BBQ.

A

Lake + watershed views · North Bend

Rattlesnake Ledge

A forested climb to broad views over Rattlesnake Lake, the Cedar River watershed, Mount Si, and Mount Washington. 4.0 miles round trip · 1,160 feet gain · moderate. Popular and exposed at the ledge, so an early start is worthwhile.

Trail details ↗
B

Rocky summit + valley views · North Bend

Little Si

A shorter, steeper-feeling forest hike with boulder fields, rock outcrops, and a clear-day view toward Mount Si and Mount Washington. 3.7 miles round trip · 1,300 feet gain · moderate. The Boulder Garden turnoffs make it easier to shorten or vary the route.

Trail details ↗
C

River + waterfalls · Gold Bar

Wallace Falls

A lush Central Cascades walk beside the Wallace River, ending at a series of three major falls. 5.6 miles round trip · 1,300 feet gain · moderate. Lower and Middle Falls are worthwhile turnaround points; the park warns that weekend parking often fills by 11 a.m.
